# Prompts

`@ai-moves/prompts/triage`, `/review`, `/deep`, `/polish`, and `/translate` render structured generation requests from program-selected inputs, each with a strict model-facing schema. Pass the rendered request to `LlmClient`, and use its `promptVersion` in the analysis key. Bump the matching value in `@ai-moves/prompts/versions` whenever a template or its output contract changes.

- **HN relevance** (`/relevance`, `HN_RELEVANCE_PROMPT_VERSION`) labels a batch of Hacker News stories `core`, `adjacent`, or `unrelated` from their titles and links in one request on the triage route. The reader is an engineer at a financial company who adopts new technology eagerly. `core` is AI itself (AI models and what happens to them, agent harnesses and AI coding tools, using AI for productivity, AI in company work, how AI is changing work, teams, companies, and society, and high-quality research on it); `adjacent` is other engineering worth knowing (software engineering and developer tooling including notable new features, AI research, security incidents with engineering substance or wide consequences, industry or market shifts); `unrelated` is everything with nothing to learn, such as personal or gossip angles on people and companies. It writes no analysis row.
- **Triage** decides pass or reject; `normalizeTriageOutput` drops the model-only null rejection reason on a pass, and a reject must carry a reason. Changelog candidates only.
- **Submission review** (`/review`, `SUBMISSION_REVIEW_PROMPT_VERSION`) scores a `score`-mode submission 0-100 against the desk's focus and writes a one- or two-sentence comment in the output language (aim for at most 80 Japanese or 140 English characters). Pieces on how AI is changing work, teams, companies, and society, and high-quality research on it, count as AI-focused. Bands: 80-100 AI-focused with concrete substance; 60-79 AI-focused but thin, or software engineering, security, or industry pieces with concrete substance; 30-59 thin or generic pieces outside AI; 0-29 gossip, promotion, or neither an engineering angle nor a bearing on how AI changes work. The submissions page shows these topics and bands from each locale pack's `desk.submissions.criteria`; change both together. `normalizeSubmissionReviewOutput` cuts a comment that overruns the contract's 300 characters. The program derives pass or reject from `push.submission.min_review_score`. Like `SUBMISSION_DEEP_PROMPT_VERSION`, its version is separate from `PROMPT_VERSIONS`, which are never bumped for these.
- **Deep** selects the HN, changelog, or submission schema by item kind and returns a contract assessment directly. It writes every prose field in the requested output language, names the reader's products from `profile.products`, asks changelog points for a category (breaking, new, change, fix), and, when a prior reading is supplied, updates it instead of starting over. A submission is briefed from the reader's excerpt as the source: points carry no category, importance runs 1 to 5 as for a Hacker News story, and `community_view` is null.
- **Polish** rewrites a finished summary and its points in place; the number of points is pinned and every field keeps its length limit.
- **Translate** returns only translated prose in the same positions. Quotes, citation metadata, links, and alert metadata stay program-owned, and the consumer checks fidelity before using the result.

Source and profile data are serialized separately from the system instructions. Templates make no outbound decisions and give the model no tools.

Templates use English instructions. [Locale packs](../locales/README.md) provide language names, natural-writing rules for deep analysis, and citation examples for polishing. Analysis uses all registered citation prefixes to remove leaked citations regardless of document language.
